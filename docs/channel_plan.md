# Acquisition channel plan - Al Tilal Al Sharqiya Auction

**Assumptions:** total budget **SAR 150,000** over the **4 weeks before the auction**. The auction is an electronic
Infath auction (Soum platform) of 11 assets in Dammam and Al Khobar: 2 commercial buildings, 2 residential buildings,
4 villas, 2 commercial land plots and 1 apartment. Buyers range from families (villas, apartment) to investors and
companies (buildings, land). Not a live campaign; no ads were run.

**Goal:** registered, financially ready bidders, not raw leads. North-star KPI: **cost per P1 lead**
(P1 = ready funds, known asset, intends to bid in this auction), measured through the qualification system.

**Weekly pacing:** week 1 heavier on Snapchat/Meta awareness (build retargeting pools); weeks 2-4 shift budget
toward Google Search and retargeting; final week before bidding closes = retargeting + WhatsApp follow-up only.
Budget is reallocated weekly toward the channel with the lowest **cost per P1**, not the lowest CPL.

## Why these three

- **Search** captures existing demand at the lowest waste; auction and Infath terms signal strong intent.
- **Meta** has the best lead-form tooling and interest/lookalike targeting to reach buyers before they search.
- **Snapchat** gives very high reach in Saudi Arabia; sending it to WhatsApp feeds the Arabic qualification agent, where most Saudi buyers already prefer to talk.

## Ruled out (for this auction and budget)

- **TikTok / X:** reach is good but purchase intent for multi-million-riyal assets is low; weak lead quality at this budget.
- **LinkedIn:** relevant only for companies buying buildings or land; very high CPC. Phase-2 option for corporate buyers.
- **Programmatic display / YouTube:** awareness value, poor lead quality within 4 weeks.
- **Offline (outdoor, print, radio):** not measurable against cost per P1 in this timeframe.

## Measurement

UTM / campaign names flow into the lead record (`campaign`, `source`) and into HubSpot (`fana_lead_source`,
`lead_channel`), so every P1/P2 can be traced back to channel and campaign, and budget follows qualified outcomes.
